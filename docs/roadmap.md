# Roadmap

Ideas being explored next. Order and scope may change.

- **Ask by voice:** speak the doubt in Hinglish instead of typing it.
- **Instant answers for repeated doubts:** a doubt many students ask gets answered immediately.
- **Teacher in control:** teachers can approve answers, flag wrong ones and pin their own fix for a part of a lecture.
- **Revision sheets:** a one-page summary of each lecture with links to the key moments.
