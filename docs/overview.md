# Overview

## The problem

Recorded lectures are the main way many students learn, especially for competitive exams and coding. When a student gets stuck on one step, the explanation they need is usually somewhere in the teacher's own lectures, but a video can't be searched. The student either gives up, asks in a comment that may never be answered, or turns to a general chatbot that explains the idea its own way, not the way their teacher taught it.

## What it does

**For students**

- Pause the lecture and type the doubt in Hinglish or English.
- Get a short answer that follows the teacher's own method and examples.
- Every point links to the exact moment in the lecture, so one tap jumps the video there.
- If the doubt depends on an earlier lecture of the same course, the answer points to that lecture.
- If the lecture doesn't cover it, the answer says so instead of making something up.

**For teachers**

- A teacher view that shows where students pause to ask, what kind of doubts they have, and which parts of a lecture need a better explanation.

## Who it's for

- Teachers and creators who sell recorded courses.
- Coaching institutes and course platforms with large doubt backlogs.
